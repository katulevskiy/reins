# Account permissions and bounded delegations

The typed native API compiles choices to the shared versioned ABAC policy model. A newly verified Gmail account receives a durable managed-account Review baseline in the same SQL transaction as registration. Dismissing setup cannot restore legacy grants or Bypass. Reconnecting an already registered account preserves its current semantics; existing installs enter managed scope through explicit settings save. Account discovery/admission remains a separate explicit owner choice for selected currently connected agents.

Gmail search/read and recipient-bounded send support deterministic permits without a model. Every automatic permission expires within seven days and has a use budget; temporary delegations expire within one day and have equally narrow, equally expiring owner authority. Recipient sets include every To and Cc recipient. Selected message reads bind normalized exact message IDs. Suspected codes/passwords retain the existing human-review floor. Other integration account defaults are informational and read-only until reversible review/block settings and positive adapters ship; their existing grants and explicit policies remain effective. Known mandatory denies/reviewer obligations always win. New capabilities in managed accounts review by default.

Automatic execution requires an authenticated single-primary phone roster and completed authenticated account sync. Legacy or unverified primary contexts visibly disable Auto and delegation issuance; supported Ask/Block choices remain available. Viewer phones and conflicted/overflow policy history expose informational views without editable presets or modes. Owner-confirmed saves and delegation changes use aggregate CAS; pause/resume/edit never reset lifetime consumption. Revoked identities and managed-account markers are permanent.

Registering a device does not create a primary-phone roster. Enabling automatic permissions on a legacy account is an explicit setup action: confirm the owner and saved recovery information, refresh the existing roster, then call `roster_create_authorized` with a screen/session-bound owner callback only when no roster exists. The callback runs after account preparation, and account retirement cancels the prepared attempt; queued or late consent cannot configure another account. The core verifies the account secret or supplied recovery code/master password and signed roster creation with the server. An existing viewer uses the enrollment/handover flow; unavailable server support leaves automatic choices disabled.

Ask is a personal account fallback (`review_by_default`), not an unconditional review obligation. With no qualifying explicit permission, requests require review; a separately admitted, bounded permission or temporary delegation can authorize matching requests. Unknown fallback facts suppress legacy permission without vetoing an independently verified explicit permission. Permanent managed-account markers continue to prevent Bypass and old grants from reopening automatic access. A soft default cannot authorize execution, consume a budget or parent a delegation, and tenant issuers cannot create it.

`require_review` remains a hard obligation for every policy kind, including older personal account defaults. Mandatory review and Deny always override permissions and delegations. Existing signed records are never silently reinterpreted: native reasons identify saved Always Ask settings, and preview plus owner confirmation explain when explicitly saving Ask replaces an old hard default. Mandatory requirements remain unchanged. Older clients that cannot decode the new effect hold the policy state rather than treating it as an absent policy.

Managed Gmail approvals offer one-off approval from the sheet; Remember and quick approval are unavailable. Old standing-grant creation, renewal and AI grant requests are rejected with guidance to account permissions or temporary permissions, including requests parked before management was enabled. Late one-off reads do not create ineffective legacy retry grants. Existing personal accounts without managed markers retain their grants and quick flows. Account discovery/admission remains separately configurable and can still be remembered. Empty agent selections on Ask/Block explicitly cover all current and future agents; they grant no new account discovery access and can never create Auto authority.

A writer transaction re-evaluates complete policy, exact account admission and all ancestor budgets, spends each shared budget once, and inserts an exact-request Started receipt. It binds owner, requesting connection, original request digest, unique attempt UUID, executor device, verified roster epoch, policy/delegation revisions and expiry. The receipt/counters reach authenticated durable account history before an external effect, under an account-specific cross-process handover lease. The final adapter rechecks membership, account access, every policy revision and newly installed mandatory requirements. A serialized policy or decision cannot mint the non-Serde execution proof.

Policy writes and positive effects hold shared leases on a separate account sidecar file; handover holds the exclusive lease through its final role check and authority transfer. The lease uses bounded acquisition on a blocking worker and the platform suspension guard, without holding the database/persistence lock across network waits. Owner prompts happen before mutation leases. Shared writer leases allow late Deny/pause/revoke to take effect while a provider is waiting. Every owner mutation rechecks its role after acquiring the lease and immediately before the atomic CAS. A recipient that has installed handover keys still cannot execute or edit permissions until the outgoing primary's signed counter frontier has been applied and fully materialized, without gaps/forks/untrusted history. The primary sync loop pulls that history before publishing its local state, so a stale empty mirror cannot publish policy deletions. Direct synchronous Gmail registration is refused so connected defaults always use the verified, fenced asynchronous flow.

Revoking an agent commits a permanent signed/synced connection brake and removes admissions before attempting server deletion. An ambiguous server failure leaves that local brake in place. The common gate and positive admission/reservation path reject the connection; a late owner confirmation revalidates current agents and cannot recreate its authority. Snapshot restore and signed removal cannot erase the brake; a reconnect uses a new connection identity.

Receipts are grow-only replay/consumption evidence, never authority. Distinct receipts supply an additive consumption floor when concurrent branches lack a common counter base. Unsupported receipt versions and mismatched receipt identities hold positive execution. Settlement joins monotonically from Started through Unknown to known Succeeded, in either merge order. Conflicting attempts for one request converge to Hold; local outcomes cannot erase that conflict. Snapshot restore preserves existing receipt/counter floors and managed scopes. Provider invocation errors retain their use and report Unknown; no automatic retry/refund is inferred from a timeout. Existing legacy Gmail paths retain their availability with local durable replay markers and the same uncertain-outcome handling, without claiming cross-device exactly-once execution.

The native facade provides effective capability/status/reason views, controlled preset/draft factories, normalized recipient/resource options, retained-history simulation, CAS preview/save, and delegation lifecycle/usage views. Custom drafts preserve expiry and remaining quota. Simulation assumes separately admitted access and is advisory; missing/truncated retained actions are explicitly marked incomplete.

## Current boundaries

Enterprise issuer verification, organization role/quorum fulfillment, admin connection provisioning, monetary cost execution evidence and generic connector/MCP positive adapters are unavailable. Organization requirements remain held until a verified role adapter exists. Conflict/overflow history blocks positive execution and account-default editing until policy reconciliation. Execution history that cannot be verified remains readable with an explicit usage-unavailable flag; when policy history is healthy, Ask/Block and Pause/Revoke still work. Unknown outcomes and receipt history have no automatic garbage collection; reaching the unresolved-attempt bound holds further automatic execution for reconciliation. Reconciliation/UI and storage compaction are follow-up work, not permission to forget evidence.

The next rollout wave enables each remaining integration only when it has normalized trusted resource facts, atomic positive reservations, complete final invocation/disclosure checks and native default choices. Project scoping waits for authenticated project attestation. This is a rollout boundary, not a migration that widens an already managed account.
