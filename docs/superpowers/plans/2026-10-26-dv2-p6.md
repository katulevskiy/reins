# Decisions v2 · P6 Circles: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or
> superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task by task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** shared control between Reins users in Personal mode:
- **Delegate:** the owner *or* the delegate decides.
- **Co-sign:** the owner *and* the co-signer decide.
- **Guardian (cooperative, 08 R7):** the guardian must approve.
- **Watcher:** informed, no buttons.

It includes:
- invitations with a six-digit safety code;
- Circle keys pinned per 08 R8;
- sealed review packets and signed, sealed answers;
- Instant and Deferred co-sign;
- revocation;
- a **For others** section on both phones.

Throughout, the server routes metadata only (08 R9, R10).

**Architecture:**
- **Shared pure logic.**
  - `reins-proto::circle` holds:
    - wire types;
    - the arrangement record and its signatures;
    - the invitation transcript and safety code;
    - packet and answer messages;
    - verification of the peer's roster against the pinned owner key.
  - `reins-policy::circles` holds scope matching, the obligations a request gets, and `settle`, which says whether
    the votes collected so far approve, deny or still wait. Both are pure and fully unit-tested.
- **Server.**
  - New durable tables:
    - `reins_circle_invites`: one row per invitation, with its protocol steps;
    - `reins_circle_links`: routing (owner, helper, kind, state), plus each party's copy of the signed arrangement,
      sealed to that party's roster credential key;
    - `reins_circle_packets`: a packet sealed to the helper's approval device; the answer sealed to the owner's.
  - Endpoints under `/reins/api/circles/*`, the approval device only.
  - `PushKind::Circle` wakes a phone, and the long poll gains a `circles` flag.
  - The server never opens anything. It learns who reviews for whom, the deadlines and the states (08 R8 *Metadata*).
- **Phone core (`reins-core::circles`).**
  - Local tables for arrangements, peer anchors, holds (the owner's side) and reviews (the helper's side).
  - The invitation steps.
  - Routing a parked request to its obligations: packets at park time; Delegate packets only once the owner's own
    review is actually needed.
  - A **gate** at the top of `approve_claimed`: the owner's tap, or an Autopilot approval, fills the owner's slot,
    and execution waits until `settle` says Approved.
  - Answers are verified, then applied.
  - Expiry.
  - Receipts both ways in Activity.
  - A UniFFI surface for the apps.
- **Apps.**
  - Settings › Circles: people, Add, Join with a link, the safety code, Pause and End.
  - Activity › **For others**: reviews grouped by person, with the scope line; Approve and Deny with a note; the
    Watcher feed with no buttons.
  - Cards show "Needs Alex too" and "Alex approved".
  - The desktop app has nothing to show: it is not an approval device. Deferred hook answers already tell the agent
    "parked for review" (P4).

## Global Constraints

- **Authorization invariants:**
  - Mandatory deny and review win.
  - Unknown facts never widen. A request whose effects can't be read matches every effect-filtered scope, so it is
    reviewed rather than slipping out of scope.
  - Authority is proven, never claimed. A Circle member's vote counts only when its signature verifies against the
    member's current approval device. That device must come from a roster chain anchored at the owner key pinned by
    the safety code.
- **Circle obligations are hard (08 R3):**
  - No permit, promotion, task, matrix cell or Bypass fills a co-signer's or guardian's slot.
  - Bypass and Auto fill only the owner's own slot ("Bypass covers the owner's own obligations only", 02 §3 rule 3).
- **Personal mode stays end to end (08 R9, R10):**
  - The server stores only what it routes in clear: ids, the parties, kind, state, deadlines and timestamps.
  - Arrangement copies are sealed to each party's roster credential key.
  - Packets are sealed to the helper's approval device box key, and answers to the owner's.
  - Signed changes carry no scope.
- **08 R8:**
  - Packets are sealed to the helper's **approval device** only.
  - Answers are signed by the helper's approval-device signing key.
  - The owner's phone verifies three things:
    - the signer is the helper roster's **current** primary;
    - the roster chains from the anchor pinned at setup;
    - no entry after the anchor changed the owner key.
  - A recovery (a new owner key) **suspends** the arrangement until a new safety code.
  - A handover verifies through the chain.
  - Viewers never decide for others.
- **R7:** guardianship is cooperative and tamper-evident. The guardian is told when the ward ends it or its owner key
  changes. Notice periods, minor accounts and the Family tenant are P7.
- **Notifications** go out only for what the user must act on:
  - **Yes:** a review waiting for a helper ("Sam: purchase $42 waiting"); a guardian told that a guardianship ended
    or was suspended.
  - **No:** a co-signer's automatic decision, a watcher notice, a helper leaving (Activity and the list only), or the
    owner's progress ("Alex approved"). Progress updates the card without a notification.
  - This overrides 04 §6.3's watcher notifications, per the program's Global Constraints.
- **Circles need phone roles on both accounts** (a roster, so there is an owner key to pin). Without one, the Circles
  screen offers the existing primary-phone setup.
- **Builds:**
  - `source ~/Documents/dev/tmp/dv2-p6/env.sh` (`CARGO_TARGET_DIR` in the worktree; `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` and `TMPDIR`
    under `~/Documents/dev/tmp/dv2-p6/`);

  - pinned clippy: `PATH=$HOME/.rustup/toolchains/1.98.1-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu/bin:$PATH cargo clippy -p <crate> -j 4 -- -D warnings`;
  - `cargo fmt`;
  - never `/tmp`.
- **Migrations:** sqlite, mysql and postgresql agree with each other and with `schema.rs`. MySQL ids, keys and
  digests use `utf8mb4_bin`.
- **Android:** `cd android && env -u ANDROID_SDK_ROOT ANDROID_HOME=$HOME/Android/Sdk ./gradlew --max-workers=4 :app:testFullDebugUnitTest`.
- **iOS:** `scripts/ios-typecheck.sh`, with `ios/Core/Generated/reins_core.swift` regenerated.

## Review Focus

1. **One person satisfying two obligations:**
   - A delegate who is also a co-signer must not fill both the owner's slot and the co-signer's slot.
   - One answer must never count for two arrangements.
   - Tests: `circles::tests::a_cosigner_who_is_also_delegate_does_not_fill_the_owner_slot`,
     `circles::tests::one_answer_counts_for_its_own_arrangement_only` (policy).
2. **Self-approval:**
   - An invitation to one's own account is refused, by the account id and by the owner key.
   - A vote from the owner's account never fills a helper slot.
   - Tests: `circle::tests::an_arrangement_with_oneself_is_invalid` (proto) and
     `circles::tests::the_owner_never_fills_a_helper_slot` (policy).
3. **A revoked member's late signature:** once the owner's phone has the arrangement ended or paused, any answer is
   refused, whatever time it claims.
   - Test: `circles_owner::an_answer_after_the_end_is_refused` (core).
   - e2e: `revoke_stops_late_answers`.
4. **Replayed co-signs:**
   - An answer is bound to:
     - the arrangement, the packet and the request digest;
     - the owner's packet nonce;
     - its own nonce.
   - It is accepted once per packet.
   - An answer replayed for another request, packet or arrangement is refused.
   - Tests: `circle::tests::answers_bind_their_packet_and_digest` (proto) and
     `circles_owner::a_replayed_answer_counts_once` (core).
5. **A server that forges membership:**
   - An arrangement counts only with both owner-key signatures, and its owner signature must be by this account's
     own owner key.
   - A substituted roster (a new owner key, a broken chain, a signer that isn't the current primary) is refused.
   - A forged link row, packet or answer has no effect.
   - Tests:
     - `circle::tests::a_forged_arrangement_is_refused`;
     - `circle::tests::peer_roster_must_chain_from_the_anchor`;
     - `circles_owner::a_server_link_without_our_signature_is_ignored` (core).
6. **A member learning beyond the card:**
   - The packet carries only the `ReviewCard`, never the raw request or arguments.
   - Under `summary` it carries no detail.
   - The helper can't fetch the owner's tickets, roster beyond the peer chain, or other packets.
   - Tests: `circle::tests::summary_cards_carry_no_detail` (proto) and
     `circles::packets_are_only_for_their_parties` (server integration).
7. **The owner's phone offline during a Circle approval:** an answer waits on the server. The phone verifies and
   applies it on its next sync, and revalidates the ticket before executing (P4). It never executes on the server's
   word. e2e: `offline_owner_executes_on_wake`.

Rules:
- An `Arrangement` is valid only when:
  - `owner.account != helper.account` and `owner.owner_pub != helper.owner_pub`;
  - its labels are clean (the roster's `check_text` rules);
  - Instant windows are 30..=600 s and Deferred windows 60..=604 800 s;
  - an `on_expiry: Owner` is on a Delegate only;
  - a Watcher has `redaction: summary`;
  - the scope's effect names are in `SCOPE_EFFECTS`, zones in `mine|friends|work|outside`, `min_level ≤ 3`;
  - lists hold ≤ 32 items.
- `verify_arrangement` checks `helper_sig` against `helper.owner_pub` over `helper_message`, and `owner_sig` against
  `owner.owner_pub` over `owner_message`.
- `peer_keys`:
  - Finds the entry at `known.0` in `chain` and requires its hash to be `known.1`.
  - Runs `roster::verify_step` for every later entry.
  - Requires every entry from the anchor onward to have `owner_pub == pinned.owner_pub`. Otherwise it returns
    `OwnerChanged`, after verifying the chain up to the change.
  - Returns the last entry's primary.
  - A chain shorter than the anchor, or a fork, is `BrokenChain`.
- `check_answer` refuses when:
  - the arrangement, packet, digest or packet nonce differ;
  - `device_id`/`epoch`/`sig` don't match `expect.signer`;
  - `at` falls outside `[created_at - ANSWER_SKEW_SECS, deadline + ANSWER_SKEW_SECS]`;
  - the note is longer than `MAX_NOTE_CHARS` or isn't clean;
  - the signature doesn't verify.

**Interfaces (Produces):**
```rust

                       pub connection: &'a str, pub account: Option<&'a str> }
pub fn scope_matches(scope: &circle::Scope, input: &Input) -> bool;

                    pub window_secs: u64, pub on_expiry: OnExpiry }
#[derive(Default)] pub struct Obligations { pub delegates: Vec<Person>, pub required: Vec<Person>, pub watchers: Vec<Person> }
impl Obligations { pub fn is_empty(&self) -> bool /* no delegates, no required */; pub fn class(&self) -> Class /* Deferred if any reviewer's is */;
                   pub fn reviewers(&self) -> impl Iterator<Item=&Person>; }
pub fn obligations(owner_account: &str, active: &[&circle::Arrangement], input: &Input) -> Obligations;
pub struct Ballot { pub arrangement: String, pub account: String, pub vote: circle::Vote }
pub struct Votes { pub owner: Option<bool>, pub people: Vec<Ballot>, pub lapsed: Vec<String> /* arrangement ids whose window ran out */ }
pub enum Settle { Approved, Denied { by: Option<String> /* account; None = the owner */, lapsed: bool }, Waiting { owner: bool, people: Vec<String> } }
pub fn settle(owner_account: &str, ob: &Obligations, votes: &Votes) -> Settle;
```

Rules:
- `scope_matches`:
  - Every non-empty list must contain the request's value: any of `kinds`, `zone`, `connection`, `account`.
  - `min_level` needs `level ≥ min`.
  - With certainty `None`, or an `Unknown` effect kind, the effects filter matches (unknown never widens). Every
    other filter still applies.
- `obligations`:
  - Only active arrangements whose `owner.account == owner_account` and whose scope matches count.
  - Delegate goes to `delegates`; Co-sign and Guardian to `required`; Watcher to `watchers`.
  - A party whose account is the owner's is ignored (defence in depth for self-approval).
- `settle(owner, ob, votes)`, in order:
  1. Any ballot `Deny` from a person named by an obligation of **that same arrangement** → `Denied{by}`.
     `owner == Some(false)` → `Denied{by: None}`.
  2. A lapsed arrangement with `on_expiry: Deny` (any reviewer kind) → `Denied{lapsed: true}`. A lapsed Delegate with
     `Owner` is removed from `delegates`.
  3. The owner's slot is filled when `owner == Some(true)`, or a delegate approved with their own arrangement's ballot,
     **unless** that delegate is also one of the `required` people (02 §3 rule 2 and 04 Co-sign `distinct_persons`).
  4. Every `required` person needs an `Approve` ballot for **their own arrangement**. Ballots for another
     arrangement, or from the owner's account, never count (`exclude_requester` / `distinct_persons`).
  5. All filled → `Approved`, else `Waiting{owner: !owner_filled, people: missing accounts}`.
  - With `ob.is_empty()`, `settle` is only `owner`'s answer.
- Test helper `SCOPE_EFFECTS_MATCH`: `circle::SCOPE_EFFECTS` equals the serde names of every `EffectKind`.

Rules:
- Every Circle call that decides or signs calls `require_decider()` (the approval device).
- `circles_available` is false without a roster, or when the server answers 404.
- **Owner create:**
  - Needs a roster and the owner seed. It draws a nonce, computes the commitment over (account, owner_pub, epoch),
    posts it, and stores `OpenInvite{draft, nonce}` before posting.
  - The link is `reins-circle:<server host>/<token>`. The apps also take a pasted `https` form.
- **Owner poll:**
  - On `challenged`:
    - It verifies the helper's roster through `peers/<invite>/roster`, using `peer_keys` with the anchor at the
      helper's challenged epoch (the code pins `helper_pub`).
    - It pins the first challenge it saw. A different later challenge is refused and the invite cancelled.
    - It builds the `Arrangement` (parties pinned at those epochs and entry hashes).
    - It seals `SealedTerms{arrangement}` to the helper's primary box key, and reveals.
  - It returns `Compare{code}`.
  - On `accepted`, it opens the helper's signed arrangement with its box key and checks:
    - that it equals the stored arrangement;
    - the `helper_sig`.
- **Owner confirm:**
  - The app checks hold + biometric through the existing `OwnerCheck`, as for loosening.
  - The core signs `owner_message`, seals copies to both parties' credential keys, posts `activate`, and stores the
    arrangement locally as active.
- **Helper open:**
  - Parses the link, posts `join` and verifies `InviteOpened`.
  - Fetches the owner's roster through `peers/<invite>/roster` and requires its latest entry's `owner_pub` to be the
    invite's.
  - Draws a nonce and posts the challenge, keeping `JoinState{nonce, opened}` before posting.
- **Helper poll:** on `revealed`, it checks the commitment against the revealed nonce, opens `sealed_terms` with its
  box key and validates the arrangement. The arrangement must name:
  - this account as the helper, with this phone's current owner key and the challenged epoch;
  - the owner as the invite's owner, with the committed key.
  It then computes the code.
- **Helper accept:** signs `helper_message` with the owner key, seals it to the owner's primary box key, posts it,
  and stores the arrangement as `pending_activation`. It becomes active when `links` shows it with a valid
  `owner_sig`.
- **`circle_people(refresh)`:**
  - Syncs `links`. For each sealed copy, it opens it with the credential key and accepts it only if:
    - `verify_arrangement` passes;
    - **our own side's `owner_pub` is our roster's current owner key** (a server can't plant a link we never signed);
    - the other party's key is pinned.
  - It applies `SignedChange`s verified against the changer's pinned owner key.
  - A local `ended` is never re-activated by the server.
  - A peer whose `peer_keys` gives `OwnerChanged` is `suspended`.

## Where the spec didn't fit (decisions made here)

- **Watchers get no push notifications.** That follows the program constraint and the coordinator's instruction;
  04 §6.3 had them. They get a quiet feed in For others.
- **Who fills the owner's slot.** The owner's own review in a Circle request follows the owner's usual policy: a tap,
  Auto or Bypass. Co-signers and guardians add slots that only they fill. The Delegate's approval fills the owner's
  slot, unless the delegate is also a required co-signer or guardian. This reads 02 §3 rule 2 ("obligations add
  up") together with 04 §1.
- **`exclude_requester`** has no separate meaning in Personal: the requester is always the owner's own agent. It is
  enforced as "the owner's account never fills a helper slot".
- **Packets go one per arrangement**, so one person with two arrangements answers twice. That is the literal 02 §3
  rule 2.
- **Redaction `custom`** (04 §5) isn't built. `full` adds subject and recipients only, never an email body.
- **Changing a scope** (widen or narrow) isn't built. End it and invite again. Pause, End and Leave are built.
- **Guardian notice periods and minors** are P7. Guardianship here is cooperative (R7). The guardian learns when it
  ends, or when the ward's owner key changes (suspended).
- **Both accounts need phone roles** (a roster) for an owner key to pin.
- **The inviter's name is the server's account email**, shown only as a label. Trust comes from the safety code.
- **The peer's latest roster comes from the server.** Withholding newer entries can't forge a signer, because the
  anchor and the epoch only move forward. It can only delay a handover's effect, and the old primary's signatures
  then still count until the owner sees the newer entry. This is accepted and stated in `security-model.md`.

## As built (changes from the plan above)

- **Review round on the protocol** (coordinator findings, each with a test):
  - `LinkChange` carries `seq` (strictly increasing per link) and `arrangement` (SHA-256 of the signed body);
    `SignedChange::verify(key, arrangement, last_seq)` refuses a replay (`link_changes_are_not_replayed`).
  - Packets and answers name the signer's roster `entry_hash` (and epoch); `Signer` refuses an older entry (a replaced
    phone) and a newer one than the verified chain reaches (a stale chain); `peer_keys(…, seen_epoch, …)` refuses a
    head older than any epoch seen (`peer_roster_must_chain_from_the_anchor`, `answers_bind_their_packet_and_digest`).
  - Card and notice text is checked (lengths; no control, bidi or invisible characters; newlines only in `detail`),
    and `ReviewCard::cleaned()` is what the owner's phone signs.
  - `scope_matches(kind, scope, input)`: an unreadable request (certainty `None` or an `unknown` effect) is inside
    every co-signer's, guardian's and watcher's effect, zone and level filter (as L3) and inside **no delegate's**
    scope; connection and account still filter. Scopes gain `max_level` (the Routine preset).
- **Auto, Bypass and Overnight fill no slot of a Circle request** (not even the owner's): the owner taps. Simpler and
  stricter than the plan's "Auto fills the owner's slot"; it reads 04 §1's Delegate/Co-sign as hard reviews (08 R3).
- **Ending or pausing** (either side) drops a delegate's or co-signer's part of what waits (the owner decides alone)
  and lapses a guardian's (denied: a ward ending a guardianship never approves what waits). An unsigned end from the
  server (an account reset) only suspends.
- **The server never stores the link secret**, not even inside the stored challenge (found by
  `invite_flow_binds_one_helper`).
- **Core tests** live in `crates/reins-e2e/tests/circles.rs` (real server, two real cores) rather than a fake-server
  harness: `invite_shows_one_code_and_activates_on_both_phones`, `cosign_instant_needs_both_and_runs_once`,
  `either_deny_wins`, `cosign_deferred_is_a_ticket_and_offline_phones_catch_up`, `revoke_stops_late_answers`,
  `a_lying_server_changes_nothing` (a replayed answer, an unsigned end, a planted arrangement),
  `nobody_shares_control_with_themself`.
- **Found while testing, not P6's:** on an account with phone roles, a *managed* Gmail send (the default for a newly
  connected account) is refused by the permission runtime ("the action is blocked or its authority cannot be
  verified") before any Circle looks at it. The e2e tests connect Gmail the pre-upgrade way.
