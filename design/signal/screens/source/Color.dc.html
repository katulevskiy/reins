Grain
One tileable grey noise texture (360 px, shown at 180 px for sharp retina grain), blended soft-light over the field. Ships as an image asset on every platform, not a live filter. It never touches paper, text or glyphs, keeps contrast within 0.1 of the flat field, and is drawn once per field, not animated. Off under Reduce Transparency.
