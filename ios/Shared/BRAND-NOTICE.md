# Provider logos

The SVG files in this folder are the brand marks of the AI products they name (Claude, ChatGPT/OpenAI, Gemini, Grok,
Hermes, Perplexity, Mistral, DeepSeek, Copilot, Cursor, Qwen, Kimi, Meta AI, Ollama). They are copied unmodified from
the `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` package (MIT licence, https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons). The marks themselves remain
trademarks of their owners and are shown only to identify which AI a connection belongs to.

`../services/gmail.svg` is the Gmail mark from Simple Icons (CC0 1.0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), drawn in
Gmail's brand colour. Gmail is a trademark of Google LLC.

`../services/telegram.svg`, `github.svg`, `gitlab.svg`, `codeberg.svg`, `bitbucket.svg`, `gcalendar.svg` (Google Calendar)
and `vault.svg` (the Bitwarden mark, for the Vaultwarden password vault) are from Simple Icons (CC0 1.0), unmodified. The
marks are trademarks of their owners and are shown only to identify the service. `gcontacts.svg`, `device_calendar.svg`,
`device_contacts.svg`, `sms.svg` and `mcp.svg` (an MCP server the user added) are plain glyphs drawn for Reins.
